N4 GRAMMAR GAMES

Grammar practice sessions

Interactive drills for the N4 patterns that need fast recognition and sentence control. Sessions 01 through 10 are ready.

01

Comparison patterns

より、ほど…ません、より…のほう、と…と…どちら

Build and recognize comparison sentences from both directions.

02

Time and simultaneous actions

ながら、ところです、まで、までに

Practice what is happening now, what happens at a deadline, and what continues until a limit.

03

Invitations and offers

ませんか、ましょう、ましょうか

Practice inviting someone, suggesting a shared action, and offering help.

04

Ability and perception

〜(られ)ます、〜ができます、見えます、聞こえます

Practice ability, permission-like possibility, and what naturally comes into view or earshot.

05

Experience and occasional events

〜たことがあります、〜ことがあります

Practice past experience and things that sometimes happen without mixing the two forms.

06

Permission and obligation

〜てもいいです、〜てはいけません、〜なくてもいいです、〜なければなりません

Practice what is allowed, forbidden, unnecessary, and required without mixing the meanings.

07

Desires and hopes

〜がほしいです、〜たいです、〜ほしがります、〜たがります、〜といいです

Practice wanting things, wanting to do actions, third-person desire, and hoped-for situations.

08

Appearance, visible emotion, and unchanged state

〜そうです、〜がっています、〜がります、〜まま

Practice outside appearances, third-person feelings, general tendencies, and states left unchanged.

09

Reasons and causes

〜から、〜からです、〜ので、〜て、〜くて、〜で

Practice direct reasons, polite explanations, emotional causes, apologies, and thanks.

10

Purpose and intended outcomes

〜に、〜ため(に)、〜ように

Practice movement goals, intentional purposes, and desired outcomes without mixing the forms.

READING HELP

Reading help

Six training modes that build the habits you need for long reading passages: spotting stacked grammar, finding sentence breaks, separating what was said or thought, tracking who did what, reading narrator mood, and checking short-passage meaning.

1

Grammar stacking

Peel apart long verb endings from three angles: pattern order, hidden base verb, and the meaning role of each grammar layer.

買う → 買ってもらえるかもしれない
l + i + d

2

Sentence breaks

Train chunking from three angles: place the slash, find the main clause, and name what the connector is doing.

雨なので/今日はうちで映画を見ます。

3

Said & thought

Train quoted/thought content from four angles: mark the span, identify who reports it, classify the reporting verb, and read the quote's force.

友だちは私に「おはよう」と言った。

4

Who did it?

Level 1 agent tracking. Read benefactive, passive, and causative sentences, then choose the doer, receiver, grammar clue, or role summary.

妹は私に荷物を持ってもらいました。
Who carried it?

5

Narrator mood

Level 1 mood tracking. Decide whether the writer's evaluation is positive or negative, then identify the feeling, clue, and clue type.

友だちが手伝ってくれました。
Positive or negative?

6

Short passages

Read compact N4-style notices, messages, and diary entries. Answer detail, reason, sequence, and inference questions without translating every word.

図書館は工事のため、来週の月曜日まで休みです。
Why is it closed?
