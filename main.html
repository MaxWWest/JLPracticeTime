READING HELP

Reading help

Three short training modes that build the habits you need for long reading passages: spotting stacked grammar, finding sentence breaks, and separating what was said or thought.

1

Grammar stacking

A verb has had one to four grammar patterns added in a row. Enter the letters of the patterns, in order, using the table.

買う → 買ってもらえるかもしれない
l + i + d

2

Sentence breaks

Long sentences are easier to read in chunks. Tap between words to place a “/” after the particles and endings that signal a break.

雨なので/今日はうちで映画を見ます。

3

Said & thought

Find what someone said, thought or wrote. Tap the start and the end of the part that belongs in quotation marks 「 」.

友だちは私に「おはよう」と言った。
